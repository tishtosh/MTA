The building blocks

Each cue works alone; combine two or three for redundancy.

1 · LIVE pill

State, not progress. Reads at a glance.

Live Updating Retrying Paused

Green = armed, blue = fetching, amber = slow/backoff, grey = paused. Pulse only in live/stale so motion carries meaning.

2 · Countdown ring

Progress without reading a number.

Ring drains as the timer runs out; becomes a spinning arc while fetching. Keep the ring decorative for screen readers.

3 · Hairline progress

Readable from across a room; zero layout shift.

retry 0:02

2–3px under the nav. Width = remaining ÷ interval; amber during backoff. No animation needed, so it's motion-safe.

4 · Updated-ago chip

Honest freshness: shows feed health, not just a timer.

Updated 4s ago Updated 48s ago Updated 2m ago

Thresholds relative to interval (green < 1×, amber < 2×, red beyond). Announce changes, not every tick.
